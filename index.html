Accuracy without interpretability is incomplete science — I build models that explain themselves.

I am Md. Faysal Hossan, a Computer Science & Engineering graduate from Daffodil International University (DIU), Bangladesh. Currently, I work as a Research Assistant at DIU, a Lead Researcher at SynthaNano Lab, and a Research Associate at Nexcure Lab. My research focuses on Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Natural Language Processing (NLP), with a strong commitment to building reliable, transparent, and deployment-ready AI systems. I work extensively with modern deep learning architectures—including Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Vision Transformers (ViTs), Mamba, and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)—integrated with Explainable AI (XAI) to solve real-world challenges in precision agriculture and healthcare diagnostics. My research has been published in premier scientific venues, including journals by Nature Portfolio and Elsevier, conference proceedings with IEEE and Springer, a book chapter with Taylor & Francis, and preprints on Zenedo. Dedicated to open science, I actively collect real-world raw data from the field and curate multiple benchmark datasets published on Mendeley Data to support reproducible research globally. Beyond research, I also serve the scientific community as an invited international journal peer reviewer and academic mentor.

Key Research Highlights:

  • Publications: Peer-reviewed articles in Springer Nature (Sci Rep, Q1), Elsevier (Data in Brief, Q1), and IEEE/Springer.
  • Open Science: Real-world raw data collection and multiple benchmark datasets published on Mendeley Data.
  • Peer Reviewer: Invited reviewer for Measurement: Food (Elsevier) and EJSRR.
  • Affiliations & Leadership: Researcher at DIU, SynthaNano Lab, Nexcure Lab; former Lead at DIU Cyber Security Club.

Research

🔬 Research Interests & Focus Domains

🌿 Agricultural AI & Plant Disease Detection

Using deep learning to detect and classify plant diseases from leaf images, monitor plant health, identify disease symptoms at an early stage, and support better agricultural decision-making.

👁 Computer Vision & Hybrid Architectures

Exploring CNN, Vision Transformer (ViT), Mamba, and Graph Neural Network (GNN) architectures for image classification, transfer learning, hybrid model development, and data augmentation.

🧠 Explainable AI (XAI)

Exploring LIME, SHAP, Grad-CAM, Grad-CAM++, Integrated Gradients, Occlusion Sensitivity, and Attention Visualization to understand and explain AI model predictions.

🔬 Medical Image Analysis

Using deep learning to analyze medical images, classify cells, and identify disease-related patterns for healthcare applications.

💬 NLP & Mental Health AI

Using transformer models such as MuRIL, BanglaBERT, and Bangla-specific language models to analyze Bangla and English text for emotion detection, mental health analysis, and other NLP tasks.
